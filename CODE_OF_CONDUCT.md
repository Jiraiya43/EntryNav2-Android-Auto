# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone who uses it or contributes to it, whatever their age, body,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to cars, Linux or Git.
- Assume good faith, and give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect the people whose cars are involved: never push someone to install something on a car they do not own.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information (vehicle identification numbers, addresses, logs with personal data)
  without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project
elsewhere. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that break it, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently
ban a participant, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the **Report a vulnerability** form of the repository's
**Security** tab, or by contacting the maintainer on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
